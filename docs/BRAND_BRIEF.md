# Brand Brief · Ikram Ansari

## Subject

Ikram Ansari. Transformation coach, entrepreneur and educator. Ten plus years coaching busy, high
performing professionals through sustainable fat loss, behavioural change and lifestyle redesign.
His method is called The Total Transformation Protocol. He is not a bootcamp trainer, not a diet
seller, not a motivational hype figure.

His gym brand is **fitoholix Fitness & Nutrition** — the signage visible throughout the film footage
is his own identity, confirmed by the client on 2026-08-16. It may read as brand reinforcement.

## Audience

Working professionals, entrepreneurs and corporate employees, men and women, roughly 25 to 45.
Time poor, information saturated, and they have failed at this before. They are sceptical of
transformation marketing specifically because they have bought it once already.

## The single job of the page

Move that sceptic from cold attention to a booked consultation, using one continuous scroll driven
film that makes a sustainable transformation feel achievable rather than sold.

## The emotional contract

Nobody arriving on this page should feel judged, rushed or shamed. There is no urgency device, no
countdown, no scarcity, no fear language, no before and after body morph animation, no implication
that their current body is a failure. **Credibility is the only persuasion mechanism on this page.**

## Voice rules

- Calm authority in a loud market. No shortcuts promised, none needed.
- No em dashes, no en dashes, no hyphens in prose anywhere in the UI. Firm client preference.
- Every string on the page comes verbatim from the §5 copy deck in BUILD_PROMPT.md, stored in
  `src/content/copy.ts`. No paraphrasing, no shortening, no additions.
- Bracketed values ([CLIENT COUNT], testimonials, contact details) render visibly as brackets and a
  build guard fails production builds while any `[` remains in testimonial data. Never invent a
  testimonial, name, profession, duration or result.
- Station 6 describes a fit, not a standard. Nothing may read as a filter that excludes or judges.

## Claims discipline

The only claims made: ten plus years coaching, zero fad diets, and whatever real numbers the client
supplies to replace the brackets. Nothing else is asserted anywhere on the page.
